# Governance proposal

## Structure

Create a legally independent nonprofit with an asset lock and mission clause. No equity, sale, acquisition, dividends, token, advertising business, or conversion to for-profit without dissolution and transfer to a compatible public-interest body.

## Bodies

- Board: fiduciary, finance, executive oversight; user-elected seats plus independent expertise.
- Algorithm and Research Council: methods, preregistration, evidence grading, algorithm changes.
- Safety and Ethics Council: survivor-informed safety, inclusion, privacy, research ethics; veto over launches posing material harm.
- Community Assembly: proposals, user priorities, elected representation.
- Independent ombudsperson: complaints about moderation, research, governance, or conflicts.

Membership, appointment, term limits, conflicts, recusals, compensation, minutes, attendance, votes, and removal processes are public. Safety-sensitive minutes may be redacted with reasons.

## Algorithm changes

Ordinary change: public proposal for at least 30 days, evidence and impact assessment, executable reference implementation, council approval, and recorded board ratification. Material changes to objectives, protected-class handling, experiments, or data sources also require community consultation and Safety/Ethics approval.

Emergency change: limited to preventing demonstrable harm, expires after 30 days unless ratified, and receives a public postmortem.

## Finance

Publish annual audited accounts, quarterly revenue/expense summaries, donors above a low threshold, grant terms, vendor contracts where lawful, compensation bands, reserves, and donation concentration. No donor receives product ranking, data, research veto, board control outside public bylaws, or privileged access.

## Contribution model

AGPL software; DCO or lightweight CLA that cannot relicense contributions into proprietary software. Security reports follow a private disclosure path. Research and safety changes require domain review, not code review alone.
